# My training stack

One machine, two NVIDIA GPUs, and a small **K3s** cluster. Kubernetes runs the
jobs; **Kueue** decides when they fit. Images, checkpoints, and supporting
services stay on the same host.

## Two GPUs, one shared budget

The training queue has **2 GPUs, 10 CPU cores, and 64 GiB RAM**. These are admission
quotas, not the host’s total capacity. Each job must fit all three resources;
the GPUs are not time-shared or split with MIG.

<StackBudget client:load />

High-priority jobs can preempt low-priority jobs. Kueue deletes the old pod and
later restarts its command. PPO and MAPPO resume from their latest saved
checkpoint; work since that checkpoint is lost.

**Headlamp** inspects workloads and logs; **KueueViz** shows queue admission.
The training-jobs viewer links to Ray dashboards, saved timelines, and Grafana.
Browser access uses localhost port-forwards on **8080–8085**, forwarded through
SSH or the IDE when remote.

## What survives a job?

Checkpoints in **`/runs`** survive pod replacement and Job deletion. MLflow,
Prometheus, Grafana, and Tempo have their own persistent volumes. Container-local
files and in-memory CodeGym episodes disappear with their pods.

All of those volumes live on this one machine. They provide persistence, not a
backup; durable checkpoints need a separate copy.
