# Hello, world

What does a circle sound like? Let's start with something simpler:
watch a point move around one, and follow its height.

## One idea, two pictures

The point moves around a circle of radius $A$. Its position at angle $\theta$ is:

$$
x = A\cos\theta, \qquad y = A\sin\theta.
$$

Plot just the height against the angle, and you get a sine wave.
One complete turn is $2\pi$ radians.

## Give it a turn

Press **Play**, or move the angle slider yourself. Change the amplitude to make
both the circle and the wave taller. The two orange dots always have the same height.

## A little more math

If the point makes $f$ turns per second, its angle is $\theta = 2\pi ft$:

$$
y(t) = A\sin(2\pi ft).
$$

Amplitude controls the height; frequency controls how quickly the pattern repeats.
That same simple shape shows up in sound, pendulums, and signals.
